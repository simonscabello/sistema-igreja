# General

## Zinc dark theme, not teal canvas
Dark theme is traditional zinc (canvas #09090B, surface #18181B, line #27272A), not SIB teal. Primary token #3B82F6 is for focus/selection only. Primary actions use high-contrast fill (ink on light, zinc-100 on dark) via actionFillClass in Button.tsx. Keep entrada/saida for financial meaning.
